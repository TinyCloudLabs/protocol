# Spaces

The autonomic space primitive, its addressing grammar, system spaces, and hosting.

## Concepts

- [Autonomic Space](autonomic-space.md) — The sovereign data primitive, created lazily when a delegation carries a tinycloud.space/host ability.
- [URI / Addressing Grammar](uri-addressing-grammar.md) — Wire-level resource grammar tinycloud:pkh:eip155:{chain}:{addr}:{space}/{service}/{path} and the ln: short form.
- [System Spaces](system-spaces.md) — Reserved spaces: default, public, account, secrets, applications, plus the synthetic encryption label.
- [Space Hosting](space-hosting.md) — Lazy space creation and host delegations carried by the tinycloud.space/host ability.
